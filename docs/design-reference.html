Title

One candle, one word, one instruction. Nothing else competes. The faint red/blue edge on the type is tape bleed, not decoration.

The main button speaks in the camcorder's voice. Everything else uses sentence case.

Tape shelf

Levels are physical objects. Each tape has a handwritten sticker; candles earned sit on the spine. The only red sticker is today's Daily Tape.

Locked tapes are dimmed, not hidden — players should see there is more.

In-game

Your face exists only where candlelight touches it. This mock shows INK mode (contour lines only). In VHS mode the same light falls on your real, graded video.

The amber line is the terrain. Bright segments are walkable; the eyes are holes.

The lips icon at bottom right stays dim while your mouth is closed and glows red when the Maw is open — the only other use of red besides REC.

Results

The stamp is the punchline. Kept still, The Maw awoke or Lights out, set like an evidence stamp over a paused tape.

Candles light one by one. The stats are written as the Wicks would say them, not as a scoreboard.

The copied text is the Wordle-style share: one symbol per Wick, in the order they emerged.
